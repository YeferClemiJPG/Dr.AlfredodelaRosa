### Prompt del banner

Use case: identity-preserve. Edit the supplied portrait of Dr. Alfredo de la Rosa into a premium medical editorial website biography banner, landscape aspect ratio 2:1. Preserve this exact man's facial identity, age, gray hair, expression, black suit, tie, posture and anatomy. Convert ONLY the man into refined photographic grayscale. Place his full head with generous breathing room and shoulders and upper torso on the LEFT, within x=6%-43% of the canvas; never cut off the top of his head. Extend the background seamlessly across the full width with generative fill: deep dark petrol teal #102e30, very subtle satin texture, a discreet soft champagne-gold light near outer edges. The RIGHT 54% must be empty dark uniform teal negative space for live HTML typography. Editorial soft lighting, formal elegant cinematic photograph, credible and restrained. No text, letters, logo, watermark, frames or additional people. Fully opaque background. The output will be used as a full-width rounded website banner with live title and biography over the right side.

### Prompt de las ondas

Use case: stylized-concept. Generate an elegant abstract background image for the contact section of a premium medical professional landing page, landscape 16:9. Flowing sculptural satin and liquid-metal waves in deep petrol teal, muted jade green, luminous ivory pearl, with very fine champagne gold rims, sophisticated realistic 3D material and soft studio light. Sweeping layered broad folds run diagonally from upper right to lower left, rich dimensional reflections, refined luxurious surface. Keep upper left mostly light ivory and calm for a dark section heading. Cards with translucent liquid glass will overlay the central area so the forms must be broad and uncluttered. Preserve a light airy impression with pearl highlights and deep teal edges, no cobalt blue, no purple. No text, logos, icons, symbols, people or watermarks. Full bleed opaque composition.
